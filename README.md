# Hi, I'm Ibrahim Shimul

I am a UX designer and product builder in Bangladesh. I design products and I build them too, using AI for both design and development. I work on client projects and I also build my own.

## How I think

1. **Understand the user.** UX research and user flows, to learn who the user is and what they need.
2. **Define the problem.** Name the user's problem, the main problem and the business problem before I draw anything.
3. **Explore options.** Compare two or three ideas and their trade-offs, and choose one with a reason.
4. **Build.** Prototypes, design systems and working products, made with AI.
5. **Hand off and improve.** Handoff pages for developers, then test and fix.

I use AI and voice input as a thinking partner. It helps me work faster and discover new things. The decisions stay mine.
